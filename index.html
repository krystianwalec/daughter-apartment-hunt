How commuting actually works in fall 2026

Do not plan using a 2024 bus map. The east-west spine changed this year.

2 Line is real

Sound Transit opened the Crosslake Connection on 28 March 2026 — light rail across I-90 between Bellevue / Mercer Island / Judkins Park and International District/Chinatown, completing the 2 Line. Trains, not the old Bellevue–Seattle freeway bus, are the trunk.

ST 550 is gone

Route 550 was discontinued 29 August 2026 with the fall service change. Riders were told to use the 2 Line. Do not screenshot an old 550 timetable into a roommate spreadsheet.

Voyager — unconfirmed

There is no confirmed “Voyager” Kirkland–Seattle / Interbay service you can set an alarm by. Kirkland’s funded BRT path is RapidRide K Line (target ~2030), Totem Lake–downtown Kirkland–Bellevue. Until something is on a published timetable, treat Voyager as rumor, not a commute.

D Line last mile

RapidRide D (Crown Hill–Ballard–15th / Interbay–Uptown–downtown) is the frequent north–south bus. It helps Greenwood and Interbay. It does not drop B at the Expedia front door from every listing: Greenwood still needs a last-mile transfer or bike; Ivy / ION are the walkable cluster; Magnolia still needs a Magnolia-to-Interbay hop.

EV reality for roommate A

Garden apartments plus a 14-50 or 240V home charger is the cheap path. Unassigned L2 stalls in new midrise are the expensive path. Stackers are usually a dead end.

Verified or advertised

  • Bell Totem Lake — verified Level 2, about $150 EV stall. 2BR is ~$3,485+ (out of band).
  • ION Interbay — official building EV. 2BR $3,095+.
  • AVA Queen Anne — Blink L2. MFTE 2BR ~$2,028 if eligible; market $3,360+.
  • Arete — EV mentioned on the listing; confirm count, reservation rules, and whether it is L2 vs 120V.

Unknown / no

  • Most Juanita / 124th / Bridlewood / Kenmore / Bothell / Magnolia / Greenwood listings: no or unknown.
  • Polaris: unverified. Get it in email before you count it.
  • Ivy stacker $225–$315: EV install is unlikely.
  • Garden + home EV extras: +$160–$340. Midrise stall: +$380–$750.

Named places, by cluster

Only real storefronts and parks. If a listing sits in a cluster, this is the errand map — not a vibe paragraph.

Village at Totem Lake

Trader Joe’s (12520 Totem Lake Blvd NE), Whole Foods Market (12501 120th Ave NE), Cinemark Totem Lake Kirkland and XD (12600 120th Ave NE), Nordstrom Rack, Chipotle, MOD Pizza, Hen House, Dué Cucina, Stack 571, Kati Vegan Thai, Silverlake Ramen. Separate but close: QFC at 11224 NE 124th St.

Juanita Village

Walkable mixed-use at 98th Ave NE & NE 116th / 97th Ln NE: Oto Sushi, Padria Mediterranean Café, Urban Coffee Lounge, Top Pot Doughnuts, Mongolian Grill, Mezcal Grill, JB Bungalow, Spud Fish & Chips on Juanita Dr. Groceries: QFC Juanita at 14130 Juanita Dr NE (north of the village). Juanita Beach Park on the water.

Houghton / Google

Google Kirkland campus (6th St S) and Feriton Spur Park (pickleball, caboose taproom) on the Cross Kirkland Corridor. Nearby: Sarducci’s, Toshi’s Teriyaki, Bibi, Fan Tang Asian Café, Koza Turkish Grocery, Houghton Beach Park / Houghton Beach Bites. Houghton Village on NE 68th is in a city redevelopment conversation — verify who is still open before you lean on it.

Downtown Kirkland

Marina Park and Lake St restaurant row, Peter Kirk Park, Kirkland Performance Center, Kirkland Urban (Google offices, QFC at 425 Urban Plaza, Shake Shack). PCC Community Markets at 430 Kirkland Way. Cross Kirkland Corridor through town. Arete sits on Central Way in this cluster.

Kenmore / Bothell

Safeway Kenmore at 6850 NE Bothell Way. ST Express 522 is the Seattle-bound bus spine from Kenmore. Bothell PCC is at 22621 Bothell Everett Hwy (Canyon Park — a drive from Hillside, not a stroll). St. Edward / Log Boom parks are the recreation, not the grocery.

Magnolia Village

Metropolitan Market Magnolia (3830 34th Ave W), W McGraw shops: Mondello Ristorante, Pink Salt, Slice Box / Queen Margherita, Petit Pierre Bakery, Uptown Espresso, Serendipity Café, Magnolia Farmers Market (seasonal Saturdays).

Interbay

Expedia HQ at 1111 Expedia Group Way W. Whole Foods Interbay (2001 15th Ave W). RapidRide D on 15th Ave W. ION and Ivy are the walk-to-HQ cluster; AVA is Lower Queen Anne with a bike/bus last mile.

Greenwood Ave

Corridor grocery: Fred Meyer Greenwood (100 NW 85th St), nearby Safeway on Greenwood. QFC on Holman Rd NW. PCC Green Lake Aurora (7504 Aurora Ave N) is a short hop, not on Greenwood itself. D Line is the frequent bus; last mile to Expedia remains real from 81st and from 125th.
